01 / The Problem
Social media wasn't supposed
to be a permanent record.

Every post you've ever made exists forever. That pressure shapes what people share — and what they don't. The result is a curated, inauthentic internet.

The Problem

  • Performance anxiety: every post is a permanent portfolio piece
  • Content hoarding: 60% of users draft posts they never publish
  • Algorithmic decay: old content resurfaces out of context
  • Digital footprint anxiety among Gen Z (78% report concern)
  • Engagement driven by vanity metrics, not genuine connection
  • Surveillance fatigue: users know their data trains algorithms, targets ads, and builds profiles they never consented to
  • Trust erosion: 72% of users feel platforms know "too much" about them

The Opportunity

  • BeReal proved demand for authentic, time-limited sharing
  • Stories (24hr) are the most-used feature on Instagram
  • No platform has made ephemerality the entire product
  • $200B+ social media market with room for new paradigms
  • Gen Z wants to share more, not less — just without consequences
  • No deleted data = nothing to track, no algorithm to manipulate you, no ad profile to sell. Privacy by architecture, not by policy.
02 / The Product
Your daily broadcast
to the world.

ThisIsIt is a full social platform — feed, follows, likes, comments, DMs, video, photo — with one fundamental rule: everything resets every 24 hours.

03 / Core Features
Built for the now.
⏱

The 24-Hour Cycle

Every piece of content — posts, comments, likes, DMs — is permanently deleted at the 24-hour mark from when it was created. No archives. No data retention.

⚡

Daily Broadcast

Post photos, videos, text, or go live. No limit on posts per day. Share your day as it happens — raw, real, and pressure-free.

👥

Follow Graph

Follow friends and creators. Your feed shows today's posts from people you follow. Follower counts persist — content doesn't.

💬

Ephemeral Reactions

Like, comment, and react. But when the post goes, so do all interactions. No comment history. No like count accumulation over time.

🔁

Reposts

See something great? Repost it to your feed. The repost still deletes at 24 hours. Repost counts are the organic trending signal — no algorithm needed. High-repost creators surface naturally in Discover.

🌐

Discover Today

Trending content ranked by reposts, not an algorithm. The most-reposted content today surfaces here. Everything on Discover is happening right now.

🔒

No Screenshots

Built-in screenshot detection notifies the poster. DRM-protected video playback. The content is meant to be experienced, not captured.

🚫

No Algorithm. No Tracking.

If the data doesn't exist, it can't be tracked. No behavioral profiling, no ad targeting from your content, no engagement-bait ranking. Your feed is chronological — just today's posts from people you chose to follow.

🛡

Privacy by Architecture

This isn't a privacy policy — it's a technical guarantee. Content is deleted from servers. There's nothing to subpoena, nothing to breach, nothing to sell. Third-party audited deletion verification.

🔢

Public & Private Channels

Public channel: your broadcast to the world. Private channel: invite-only, for family, close friends, or tight groups. Same 24-hour rule, different audiences. Share your kid's first steps with grandma — not the internet.

03b / Who It's For
Two audiences.
One product.

ThisIsIt serves different needs for different people. The early adopters and the expansion market want the same thing — freedom from permanence — for very different reasons.

Primary: Gen Z (18–27)

Early Adopters — Launch Target

  • Already prefer ephemeral formats (Stories > Feed posts)
  • Highest digital footprint anxiety of any generation
  • Want to post more, but self-censor because permanence feels risky
  • Burnt out on algorithmic feeds and "personal brand" culture
  • Adopt new platforms fast — drove BeReal, TikTok, and Threads early growth
  • Primary use: Public channel. Daily broadcast. Creator subscriptions.

Expansion: Millennial Parents (28–38)

Growth Market — Post-Launch

  • Share photos/videos of their kids constantly but worry about building a digital footprint their child never consented to
  • Want to share moments with family — not archive them on the public internet
  • Private channels are the killer feature for this demo: share the first steps with grandma, not with the algorithm
  • Less interested in public broadcast, highly motivated by intimate sharing
  • Higher willingness to pay for privacy-first products
  • Primary use: Private channels. Family sharing. Ephemeral moments with close circle.
NOT THE TARGET (AND THAT'S OK)

People who post once a month. People who use Instagram as a scrapbook. Influencers who depend on old content going viral. People who want a permanent record. These users don't have the problem we're solving — and building for them would dilute the product.

07 / Business Model
Monetization without
the baggage.

★ ThisIsIt+

Premium subscription: extended post formats (longer video, higher res), custom countdown themes, profile customization, and priority placement in Discover during posting hours.

⚡ Promoted Moments

Brands can sponsor 24-hour campaigns that feel native. Ephemeral ads create urgency. Limited-time brand content that disappears — driving action, not scroll-past.

🛡 Data Promise

No ad targeting from deleted content. Our privacy-first model is the marketing. Trust is the moat. We delete content from servers — verifiably, with third-party audits.

08 / Creator Economy
Scarcity is the
monetization engine.

If you miss it, it's gone. That's not a limitation — it's what makes creators valuable. The incentive isn't a content library. It's "don't miss tomorrow."

Why Creators Post

  • Zero pressure: content disappears, so there's no permanent portfolio to curate. Post raw, post often, post without overthinking.
  • Built-in urgency: followers know content is fleeting, so engagement per post is higher. No competing with your own back catalog.
  • Subscriber revenue: creators earn recurring income from subscriber-only daily drops. The subscription isn't for a library — it's for access to tomorrow.
  • Persistent reputation: subscriber count, streak days, and total earnings persist even when content doesn't. Your track record grows while your content stays fresh.

Why Users Subscribe

  • Exclusive daily drops: subscriber-only posts that only subscribers see for 24 hours. Miss it? Gone forever. That's the value.
  • Golden Hour: live events hosted by creators, visible only to subscribers. Can't be replayed, can't be screenshotted. Appointment content.
  • Inner circle access: subscriber-only comments and reactions on creator posts. A tighter community, not a bigger one.
  • FOMO is real: when your friend talks about what a creator posted yesterday and you missed it — that drives subscriptions more than any marketing.
🌟

Subscriber-Only Drops

Creators post exclusive content visible only to paying subscribers. Still ephemeral. Still 24 hours. The subscription buys access, not permanence.

🌇

Golden Hour

Live events for subscribers only. No replay, no recording. The experience is the product. Think private concert, not YouTube video.

📈

Creator Metrics

Subscriber count, consecutive-day streak, total earnings, and engagement rate — all persist. Your reputation compounds even though your content doesn't.

09 / Trust & Transparency
When we can't delete,
we tell you why.

Ephemerality is our core promise. But legal obligations exist. Our approach: radical transparency about when and why data is ever preserved.

The Legal Hold System

When law enforcement serves a valid legal request, we may be required to preserve specific account data beyond 24 hours. Our system handles this with user-first transparency:

  • Account status model: Every account is active, legal_hold, or suspended. The 24-hour deletion job skips held accounts.
  • User notification (when allowed): "Your account is paused. Your content is being preserved under a legal request." Direct, honest, no corporate doublespeak.
  • Gag order handling: Some court orders prohibit user notification (18 U.S.C. § 2705(b)). When gagged, we show a generic suspension. When the gag lifts, we notify retroactively.
  • Minimal data surface: Like Signal, if there's nothing stored, there's nothing to hand over. A subpoena on a normal account returns at most 24 hours of content. That's it.

Quarterly Transparency Report

Published publicly every quarter. No spin. Just numbers.

  • Total legal hold requests received
  • Requests complied with vs. challenged
  • Requests that included gag orders
  • Total accounts affected
  • Average hold duration
  • Accounts notified after gag order expiry
  • Third-party deletion audit results

"We received 3 hold requests this quarter. 0 accounts were held without notification." — That's the kind of report we want to publish.
